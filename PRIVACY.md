# Privacy Policy

**In Process for iOS** ("the app") is published by xDJs LLC. It is a client for [In Process](https://inprocess.world), the service where your account and your published Moments live.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

## The short version

The app sends your email address and the photos you choose to publish to In Process. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and xDJs LLC runs no servers that receive your data.

## What the app handles

**Email address.** You enter your email to sign in. The app sends it to In Process so it can email you a one-time code. The app does not store your password, because there is none.

**Sign-in key.** After you sign in, In Process issues a key for this installation. It is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device and is removed from the service when you sign out.

**Photos.** The app asks for access to your photo library so it can read one album, called "In Process", and show its contents. It uploads only:

- photos in that album, when automatic uploading is on or after you approve them, and
- a single photo you pick yourself to publish.

It does not upload, scan or analyse the rest of your library.

**Photo metadata.** Photos are uploaded as they are, including embedded information such as the date, camera details and location, if the photo has it. Check a photo before publishing if this matters to you.

**Titles and descriptions.** Any title or description you type for a Moment is sent with it.

**Upload records.** A record of which photos have been uploaded is kept on your device so nothing is published twice. It is deleted when you delete the app.

**Notifications.** If you allow them, the app shows notifications on your device when uploads need your attention. These are created on the device; nothing is sent to a notification server.

## What is public

Publishing is the purpose of the app. A photo you publish becomes a Moment on In Process, and the photo, its metadata, and its title and description are publicly visible. Published Moments may be stored permanently and may not be removable.

## What the app does not do

- It does not track you across apps or websites.
- It does not include advertising or analytics software.
- It does not sell or share your data with anyone other than In Process, which you are signing in to.

## Who receives your data

Your email, photos and Moment details go to the In Process service and its storage providers. How In Process handles them is governed by In Process, not by this policy.

## Your choices

- You can turn automatic uploading off and approve photos one at a time.
- You can remove a photo from the album before it uploads.
- You can sign out, which removes this installation's sign-in key from the service.
- You can remove Photos access in iOS Settings at any time.
- To delete your In Process account or a published Moment, contact In Process.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
